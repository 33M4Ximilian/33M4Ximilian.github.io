你好！我是 Emilian，目前是 SZTU无人驾驶航空器系统工程专业的本科生。

我对机器人、具身智能和机器人学习很感兴趣。过去一段时间里，我接触了机器人强化学习、灵巧操作、视觉感知、运动规划、ROS 2 系统集成以及 Sim-to-Real 部署。这些经历让我能够从算法、仿真、感知、控制和真实硬件等不同层面理解一个机器人系统。

现在我仍处在学习和探索的阶段。相比快速得到一个看起来不错的结果，我更愿意花时间理解系统为什么工作、又为什么失败。我喜欢把不熟悉的问题一点点拆开，也愿意和困难的问题多相处一会儿，再慢慢把想法变成可以运行的系统。

目前，我正在继续学习机器人学习与具身智能，希望逐渐建立更扎实的理论基础，也积累更多真实系统中的实践经验。

Hi! I’m Emilian, an undergraduate student in Unmanned Aircraft Systems Engineering at SZTU.

I am interested in robotics, embodied AI, and robot learning. So far, I have explored reinforcement learning, dexterous manipulation, robotic perception, motion planning, ROS 2 system integration, and Sim-to-Real deployment. These experiences have helped me understand robotic systems from several connected perspectives, including algorithms, simulation, perception, control, and real hardware.

I am still learning and exploring. Rather than focusing only on getting a system to work once, I enjoy understanding why it works, why it fails, and how its different components fit together. I am willing to stay with difficult problems, learn unfamiliar tools, and gradually turn an idea into a working system.

Currently, I am continuing to build my foundations in robot learning and embodied intelligence while gaining more experience with real robotic systems.
